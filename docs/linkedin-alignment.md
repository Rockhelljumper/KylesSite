# LinkedIn alignment guide

This is an editorial checklist for the profile owner. It is deliberately kept
outside the public site: LinkedIn is not changed automatically by the portfolio
deployment.

## Current positioning

- Availability: Remote-only engineering leadership opportunities.
- Target roles: Platform Engineering Manager, Engineering Manager, Director
  Opportunities, Software Engineering Team Lead, and Platform Engineering Team
  Lead.
- Suggested headline: `Platform Engineering Manager | Engineering Leader |
  Reliable, compliant systems | Developer platforms, delivery, SRE & DevSecOps`
- Suggested About opening: `I build and lead platform engineering capabilities
  for teams that need reliable delivery, clear operational ownership, and
  security-conscious systems that keep moving.`

## Keep the three public surfaces in sync

1. Use the same remote-only availability and leadership-role focus on LinkedIn,
   the portfolio homepage, and the résumé landing page.
2. Feature the portfolio, the leadership-case-study page, and the leadership
   résumé. Do not feature synthetic references as though they were client work.
3. Keep the employment history scoped to claims supported by the résumé source:
   three direct reports, influence across 30+ partners, 99.999% stability, 40%
   faster deployments, 20% lower Azure cost, and recovery in under two weeks.
4. Ask for recommendations only from people who can describe specific observed
   work; do not request generic endorsements or invent quotations.

## Pre-publish review

- Confirm all numbers against the current approved résumé PDF.
- Remove customer names, confidential system details, and security-sensitive
  implementation information.
- Review location and Open To Work preferences for **Remote** only.
