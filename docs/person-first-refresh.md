# Person-first portfolio refresh

## Intent

Keep the portfolio credible, but make it feel like a living personal site rather
than a dense consulting brochure. Recover useful personal/community material
from the prior live site, connect Kyle's hobby work naturally, and make the
experience more visual and responsive without turning it into a decorative
landing page.

## Acceptance criteria

- The landing page communicates one clear professional idea without attempting
  to enumerate every capability or proof point above the fold.
- Primary navigation remains compact; non-essential professional detail moves
  behind intentional pages rather than competing for attention on the home page.
- Personal interests, community/hobby work, and a now-reading/watching/listening
  surface are present, editable from canonical content data, and visibly
  secondary to career evidence.
- The hobby/project connection is a real, verified public link and is framed as
  a human interest, not a business service.
- Photography and product imagery have a role in the page hierarchy. Motion is
  subtle, respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and does not block reading or
  keyboard use.
- Existing case-study privacy boundaries, validated claims, metadata, contact
  security, responsive behavior, and accessibility standards remain intact.
- Tests cover newly interactive or content-critical behavior, and screenshots
  capture the revised primary views.

## Deliberate non-goals

- Do not make private work, personal schedules, detailed home infrastructure,
  or sensitive operational material public.
- Do not recreate the old site literally or add motion merely to fill space.
- Do not present hobby work as a corporate offering or fabricate book, movie,
  audience, or business claims.

## Research and review boundary

- The live site provided source material for the smaller community, makerspace,
  maker-project, reading, outdoors, gaming, and podcast references. It was not
  used as a design template: its generic project cards, broad navigation, and
  dense feature lists are intentionally not restored.
- Into the Nerdverse is linked only through its verified public Spotify show
  and uses its public-facing artwork. Do not claim audience size, publication
  cadence, or a current host/founder title without confirmation.
- Books, movies, and games are described as interests rather than a current
  public list. Add specific titles only after Kyle supplies the list to publish.
- Makerspace dates and roles remain sourced from the site data and should be
  confirmed before any external organization link or new quantified claim is
  added.
- A separate hobby repository reviewed during this work contained committed
  credentials and internal topology. It is deliberately not linked, pictured,
  or named in public content.
